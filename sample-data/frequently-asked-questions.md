# Frequently Asked Questions

## How can I track an order?
Give support your order number, such as ORD-1002. We can look up the recorded order status, tracking number, estimated delivery, and delivery date.

## Can I change or cancel an order?
Contact support before the order ships. Changes and cancellations require confirmation from the team. An order that has already shipped cannot be cancelled in transit.

## What if my item arrives damaged?
Report the issue within 7 days of delivery, provide your order number, and keep the packaging. Support can open a ticket for the team to arrange next steps. Do not dispose of the item before the team responds.

## How do I contact a person?
Ask the assistant to create a support ticket. Include an order number or an email address and a description of the problem. Our team reviews support tickets during business hours: Monday–Friday, 9 AM–5 PM UTC, excluding public holidays. We aim to reply within 1 business day; this is a target, not a guarantee.
