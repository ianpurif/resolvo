# Warranty Policy

Northstar Supply products include a one-year limited warranty from the delivery date covering manufacturing defects under normal use. The warranty excludes accidental damage, misuse, cosmetic wear, unauthorized modifications, and damage from improper storage.

To request warranty service, provide an order number and describe the defect. Support will create a ticket. The team may request photos and proof of purchase. Approved claims may receive repair or replacement at the team's discretion. A warranty request is not automatically an approved claim.

Damage present on arrival should be reported within 7 days of delivery. Keep the original packaging and all parts until the support team completes its review. The warranty is separate from the 30-day return policy.
