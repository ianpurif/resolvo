# Refund Policy

Northstar Supply refunds approved returns to the original payment method. After the returned item arrives at our warehouse, inspection takes up to 3 business days. An approved refund is then issued within 5 business days. Your bank may take an additional 5–10 business days to post the credit.

Original shipping fees are not refunded for change-of-mind returns. Shipping fees are refunded when Northstar Supply sent an incorrect or defective item. A support ticket alone does not approve a refund. The support team must review the request and confirm approval.

Cancelled orders that have not shipped are refunded within 5 business days. We do not offer cash refunds or refunds to a different payment account. If a refund is overdue, contact support with the order number so the team can investigate.
