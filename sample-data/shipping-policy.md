# Shipping Policy

Northstar Supply processes orders within 1–2 business days, excluding weekends and public holidays. Standard shipping usually takes 3–5 business days after dispatch. Express shipping usually takes 1–2 business days after dispatch. Estimated delivery dates are estimates rather than guarantees.

When an order ships, a tracking number is added to the order record. Tracking updates can take up to 24 hours to appear with the carrier. Support can share the order's recorded status, tracking number, and estimated delivery date, but cannot see real-time carrier scans.

If tracking shows delivered but the package is missing, check around your address and with neighbors, then contact support. If an order is more than 3 business days past its estimated delivery date, contact support with your order number. Address changes can be requested before an order ships; the team must confirm the change.
